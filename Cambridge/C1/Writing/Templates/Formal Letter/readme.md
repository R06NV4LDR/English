# The Formal Letter / Email
| Target Reader: | A manager, a college principal, a local council member, or a magazine editor.                                                                               |
| -------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tone:**      | Objective, polite, and professional, **No contractions** (_I am_ instead of _I'm_), no slang, and heavy use of  passive structures and advanced vocabulary. |
## Structure Template:

---

### Salutation:

**_Dear Mr/Ms [Surname]_** (if you know the name)

**_Dear Sir or Madam_** (If the prompt does not )
### Paragraph 1: Reason for Writing

- State your purpose clearly using formal collocations.

	**_I am writing to you on behalf of [Group] in response to your recent advertisement regarding.._**

	**_"I am writing to express my dissatisfaction with..."_** 

### Paragraph 2: The First Point

- Dive into the first point of the prompt using natural transitions.

	**_Anyway, about your question regarding [Topic] - honestly, if I were in your shoes, I'd definitely consider..._**

	**_You might want to bear in mind that..._**

### Paragraph 3: The Second Point / Suggestions

- Add more details and keep the tone enthusiastic.

	**_Another thing worth mentioning is that..._**

	**_Whatever you do, make sure you don't miss out on..._**

### Paragraph 4: Closing Formula

- Ask them to keep you updated.

	**_Anyway, I'd better dash - let me know how it all goes!_**

	**_Drop me a line when you get a chance and tell me what you decided._**

### Sign-off:
- Just
	**_Best wishes, | All the best, | Warmly,_**
	**_[Your First Name Only]_**
