# The Report (Part 2)
**Tone:** Formal, factual, and retrospective.
**Goal:** Evaluate a past event, place, or situation (e.g., a workplace, a study program) and provide a recommendation based on facts.

## Structure Template:

---
### Report on  [Topic, e.g., The Company Internship Scheme]

### Introduction

The purpose of this report is to assess the efficacy of [Topic] and to evaluate its overall success. It is based on feedback gathered from  [employees/participants] over the past [Time Period].
### Positive Aspects

Feedback was overwhelmingly positive regarding [Aspect 1]. the vast majority of participants noted that [Detail]. Furthermore,  [Aspect 2] proved to be highly beneficial because [Reason].
### Areas for Improvement

Despite the overall success, certain shortcomings were identified. Most notably, [Problem 1]. In addition, several individuals expressed dissatisfaction with [Problem 2], citing a lack of [Detail].
### Recommendations

In order to enhance future iterations of [Topic], I would suggest the following measures:

1. [Recommendation 1]:  [Detail]
2. [Recommendation 2]: [Detail]
