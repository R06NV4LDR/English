# The Informal Letter / Email

| Target Reader: | A friend, a classmate, or a close colleague.                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tone:**      | Warm. engaging, and conversational. Use **contractions** (I'm, can't, don't), phrasal verbs, and lively punctuation (like exclamation marks). However, the vocabulary must still be sophisticated enough to prove you are a C1 student. |
## Structure Template:

---

### Salutation:

**_Dear [First Name]_. or _Hi [First Name],_**
### Paragraph 1: The Friendly Opening

- Acknowledge their last message and ask how they are doing.

	**_It was great to hear from you! I'm so sorry it's taken me a while to get back to you, but things have been absolutely hecti here lately_**

	**_I was absolutely thrilled to hear that you're planning to..._**

### Paragraph 2: Reacting & Giving the First Piece of Advice/Info

- Dive into the first point of the prompt using natural transitions.

	**_"Anyway, about your question regarding [Topic] - honestly, if I were in your shoes, I'd definitely consider..."_**

	**_"You might want to bear in mind that..."_**

### Paragraph 3: The Second Piece of Advice/Info

- Add more details and keep the tone enthusiastic.

	**_"Another thing worth mentioning is that..."_**

	**_"Whatever you do, make sure you don't miss out on..."_**

### Paragraph 4: The Warm Wrap-up

- Ask them to keep you updated.

	**_Anyway, I'd better dash - let me know how it all goes!_**

	**_Drop me a line when you get a chance and tell me what you decided._**

### Sign-off:
- Just
	**_Best wishes, | All the best, | Warmly,_**
	**_[Your First Name Only]_**
