_misses proper introduction_


Although it may be that the bypass will lead to a reduction in pollution and noise. Its not completely clear if  local businesses stay unaffected.

The tourism in town has grown over the last decade and this proposal could bring the whole movement to a sudden halt. Why would a tourist still bother to visit us, if we make it that hard for them to get here?

Of course the reduction of traffic is a welcome upgrade for the town. But considering that local traders will face significant lower customer numbers through the bypass, I'm worried that this may be a bad decision, especially for the growth of our small community in the near future. Who's gonna rent a shop in a town, where no one drives through? 
Surely I wouldn't! And I cant get rid of the feeling that I'm not the only one thinking this way.

The long term consequence of this bypass will be a shift in location: the shops will move to a neighborhood with more traffic. Employees will search for apartments near their workplace. The town could become a ghost town with no businesses at all. 

Maybe in the near future we're gonna debate about another bypass in a slightly different area. And I'll think back to this moment with a smudge on my face. Knowing that this bypass was a bad idea in the first place.
